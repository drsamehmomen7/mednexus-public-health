Public Health Report Intelligence

Turn public health reports into structured, actionable data

Paste or upload a report from a hospital or health unit. MedNexus identifies the report type, extracts the fields a surveillance team actually needs, and prepares them for statistics, dashboards, and indicators — without the report ever leaving this machine.

How it works

From a paragraph of clinical notes to a number on a dashboard

Four steps, all running on this machine — nothing is sent to a cloud model.

Paste or upload

Drop in free text from a hospital, clinic, or health-unit report — no fixed template required.

MedNexus reads it locally

A biomedical NER model plus deterministic rules pull out disease names, dates, ages, vaccines, and doses — on this machine, not a cloud API.

A reviewer confirms it

Every field ships with a confidence badge, so a human can fix anything the model is unsure about before it counts.

It feeds the indicators

Confirmed records flow straight into the case-count, coverage, and adverse-event dashboards — filterable by disease, region, and time.

Report types

Built for the reports a surveillance team actually receives

Two report types are extracting at full accuracy today; three more are designed and coming next.

Live

Notifiable Disease

Case reports for infectious diseases requiring mandatory reporting — disease, region, onset, demographics, lab confirmation.

Live

Immunization

Vaccination records — vaccine, dose number, route, patient age in months for infants, and adverse events following immunization.
